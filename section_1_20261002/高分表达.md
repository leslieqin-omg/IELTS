# IELTS Academic Writing Section 1 高分表达手册

本手册汇集本次模拟训练中 Task 1（男女比例柱状图）与 Task 2（人工智能与就业讨论）的全部核心高分词汇、搭配、句型与模板。

---

# 第一部分：Task 1 高分表达（图表分析专栏）

Task 1 核心原则：**客观陈述、准确量化、对比鲜明、绝无主观推测**。

## 1. 开头改写通用高分句型（Paraphrasing Formula）
- **通用公式 1**：`The bar chart compares the proportion of male and female students enrolled across six distinct university faculties in a European country in 2022.`
- **通用公式 2**：`The provided graph illustrates the gender distribution of undergraduates across six different academic disciplines within a particular European nation in the year 2022.`

### 核心同义替换库：
- **shows / gives information about** ➔ `illustrates`, `compares`, `delineates`, `presents data on`
- **percentage of male and female students** ➔ `the proportion of men and women`, `gender breakdown / distribution`
- **enrolled in** ➔ `studying across`, `matriculated in`, `admitted to`
- **different university faculties** ➔ `distinct academic fields / disciplines / departments`

---

## 2. Overview 概述段高阶句式（拿 7+ 分关键，不带具体数值）
- **句型 A（极度对比 In sharp contrast to）**：
  > *Overall, there was a pronounced gender divide across the six faculties. Computer Science recorded the highest concentration of male undergraduates; in sharp contrast, Education was overwhelmingly favored by female students.*
- **句型 B（让步对比 While / Whereas）**：
  > *Overall, it is evident that male students heavily dominated technical disciplines such as Computer Science and Engineering, whereas their female counterparts significantly outnumbered them in humanities and care-oriented fields, particularly Education.*

---

## 3. 数据描写与倍数/差额核心词块
- **占据主导**：`dominate`, `account for the vast majority of`, `constitute a dominant share`
- **人数超过 / 处于劣势**：`outnumber [sb]`, `lag behind`, `be significantly lower than`
- **极值描述**：`stand as the most male-dominated faculty`, `reach its peak at ...`, `hit the lowest trough`
- **大致平衡**：`Law showed a relatively balanced gender representation (46.5% vs 53.5%).`

# 第二部分：Task 2 高分表达（学术议论文专栏）

Task 2 核心原则：**因果链条严密、论证充分展开（PEEL 结构）、用词精准、句式多变**。

## 1. 核心高分词块与固定搭配（Collocations）

| 词块 / 表达 | 中文释义 | 实战高分例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **spark widespread debate** | 引发广泛争议/讨论 | The rapid advancement of AI has **sparked widespread debate** regarding the job market. |
| **routine and repetitive tasks** | 常规且重复性的任务 | Jobs involving **routine and repetitive tasks** are most vulnerable to automation. |
| **vulnerable to being replaced** | 极易被取代 | Manual laborers are **vulnerable to being replaced** by algorithmic systems. |
| **standardized procedures** | 标准化流程/固定步骤 | Workers primarily follow **standardized, step-by-step procedures**. |
| **intellectual engagement** | 智力参与/深度思考 | These positions do not require deep **intellectual engagement** or strategic judgment. |
| **face threats of displacement** | 面临被淘汰/替代的威胁 | Clerical staff **face severe threats of displacement** unless they undergo retraining. |

| **emotional intelligence / empathy** | 情商 / 同理心 | Such professions demand not only creativity but also **emotional intelligence and empathy**. |
| **put oneself in someone's shoes** | 设身处地为某人着想 | Teachers can **put themselves in students' shoes** to provide tailored emotional guidance. |

## 2. 篇章逻辑推进与高级替换库

### ① 动作与趋势进阶
- **快速发展**：`develop at an unprecedented pace`, `exponential growth`, `burgeon / mushroom`
- **广泛普及**：`widespread adoption / prevalence`, `permeate every aspect of society`
- **促进 / 推动**：`foster` (培养), `facilitate` (提供便利), `catalyze` (催化变革), `propel forward` (强力驱动)
- **提升 / 降低**：`enhance / elevate / boost` (提升) vs `mitigate / alleviate / curb` (缓解/抑制)
- **与……密切相关**：`be closely linked to`, `have a direct bearing on`, `be deeply intertwined with`

### ② 论证连接推进（摆脱 Firstly / Secondly）
- **首要论点**：`Primarily, ...`, `To begin with, ...`, `The principal consideration is that ...`
- **次要论点**：`Equally importantly, ...`, `Furthermore, ...`, `A secondary factor worthy of note is that ...`
- **再其次**：`Moreover, ...`, `Another dimension worth examining is ...`
- **与此同时**：`Simultaneously, ...`, `Concurrently, ...`, `In the meantime, ...`
- **相反/对比**：`In sharp contrast to [A], [B]...`, `Conversely, ...`, `Contrary to popular belief, ...`

### ③ 精准程度副词
- **程度严重/深刻**：`profoundly`, `substantially`, `severely`, `drastically`
- **程度轻微/边缘**：`marginally`, `negligibly`, `subtly`

---

## 3. 高分论证句式模型（Grammatical Range）

- **模型 1：精准亮出立场（宾语从句）**  
  *In my view, whether artificial intelligence displaces human workers depends largely on the nature of the profession.*
- **模型 2：分词伴随结果（因果链条，Band 8.0 杀手锏）**  
  *Automation streamlines repetitive workflows, **thereby substantially cutting** operational expenditures.*
- **模型 3：部分倒装递进（Not only does...）**  
  ***Not only does** innovative technology boost industrial efficiency, **but it also creates** entirely novel job categories.*
- **模型 4：平衡让步句（While it is undeniable that...）**  
  ***While it is undeniable that** automation causes short-term disruptions, **it must also be recognized that** it sparks long-term economic vigor.*
- **模型 5：伴随对比（Rather than doing...）**  
  *Therefore, **rather than being substituted** by machines, professionals will use AI as a tool to enhance productivity.*
