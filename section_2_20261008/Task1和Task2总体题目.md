- **Task 2:** ~40 minutes (at least 250 words)

## Task 1: Academic Report (Line Graph)

> **The graph below shows the consumption of four different energy sources in a European country from 1980 to 2020.**
> 
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

Write at least **150 words**.

## Task 2: Academic Essay (Opinion Essay)

> **Some people believe that university education should be free for all students, regardless of their financial background. Others, however, argue that students should pay tuition fees themselves because higher education primarily benefits the individual.**
> 
> **To what extent do you agree or disagree with the idea that university education should be completely free?**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least **250 words**.
