## 二、去模板化重构范文（Band 8.0 纯自然衔接版）

本篇范文**100% 保留你的核心立意与论据**（贫富差异、寒门天才奖学金全免、富家子弟自费与捐赠设立基金、大学声誉与财政双赢），彻底剔除机械连接词（`Firstly`, `Furthermore`, `Therefore`, `In this way`），全面改用**指代回指、概括性名词与内容递进**。

### 范文正文（278 词）

> In modern society, access to higher education has a profound bearing on an individual's career prospects and socioeconomic status. While the proposal to eliminate university tuition entirely has sparked widespread public debate, such a blanket policy overlooks differences in students' financial capabilities and academic merits. A differentiated approach, where tuition fees reflect both household income and scholarly promise, represents a far more equitable solution.
> 
> Higher education should be completely free for talented students from underprivileged backgrounds. In impoverished regions, limited family earnings render university attendance virtually unattainable for local youths. Left unaddressed, this financial barrier deprives intellectually gifted individuals of the opportunity to develop their academic potential and contribute to society. Such a waste of talent can be prevented only when institutions offer comprehensive scholarships and living allowances, ensuring that poverty does not obstruct upward social mobility.
> 
> Charging tuition to affluent students, on the other hand, is both justified and necessary. Fees and voluntary philanthropy from wealthy households supply the capital needed to upgrade laboratory facilities and recruit distinguished faculty. These financial contributions need not stop at campus infrastructure: university endowment funds built from alumni generosity can directly subsidize disadvantaged peers. This arrangement provides institutions with steady operational revenue while allowing them to build an academic reputation rooted in intellectual merit rather than family wealth.
> 
> In conclusion, higher education should not be made unconditionally free for all citizens. A hybrid model—one that derives financial support from prosperous families while guaranteeing full tuition waivers for promising scholars in need—strikes the ideal balance between institutional sustainability and social fairness.

## 三、去模板化衔接深度拆解（看高分文章如何“字里行间自然咬合”）
