# 第一部分：Task 1 高分表达（动态折线图专栏）

针对本次能源消费走势（1980–2020），提炼出动态图表最核心的高分表达与句式。

## 1. 动态折线图开头改写句型
- **公式 1**：`The line graph illustrates changes in the consumption of four types of energy sources in a European nation over a 40-year period from 1980 to 2020.`
- **公式 2**：`The provided chart tracks the trends in energy consumption across four different fuel categories in a specific European country between 1980 and 2020.`

## 2. Overview 概述段黄金句式（两极趋势对比）
- **句型 A（上升与下降鲜明对立）**：
  > *Overall, while coal consumption experienced a dramatic and sustained decline, natural gas and renewable energy sources witnessed substantial growth over the four decades.*
- **句型 B（榜首易主与交叉特征）**：
  > *It is noticeable that coal, initially the dominant fuel source in 1980, was overtaken by natural gas around 1995 and eventually plummeted to become the least consumed energy source alongside nuclear power by 2020.*

## 3. 动态趋势高阶动词与词块
- **急剧下跌 / 骤降**：`plummet`, `slump`, `shrink by more than threefold`, `witness a steep downturn`
- **稳步攀升 / 激增**：`surge exponentially`, `experience a steady upward trajectory`, `quadruple`
- **超越 / 超车**：`overtake`, `surpass`, `eclipse`
- **达到峰值并回落**：`peak at ... before plateauing and gradually dwindling`

# 第二部分：Task 2 高分表达（高等教育与学费专栏）

针对本次高等教育收费与财政反哺话题，提炼最权威的学术词块与论证句型。

## 1. 核心高分词块与搭配（Collocations）

| 词块 / 表达 | 中文释义 | 实战高分例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **underprivileged backgrounds** | 贫困/弱势家庭背景 | Students from **underprivileged backgrounds** deserve equal educational access. |
| **exorbitant tuition fees** | 高昂得令人望而却步的学费 | Imposing **exorbitant tuition fees** deprives poor youths of higher education. |
| **intellectually gifted individuals** | 智力出众/才华横溢的人才 | Scholarships should be preserved for **intellectually gifted individuals**. |
| **deprived of the opportunity** | 被剥夺了机会 | Poor students should not be **deprived of the opportunity** to study. |
| **hinder social mobility** | 阻碍阶层流动 | Financial barriers to college heavily **hinder social mobility**. |
| **affluent patrons / families** | 富裕的资助人 / 优渥家庭 | Universities can solicit donations from **affluent patrons**. |
| **institutional endowment fund** | 大学机构捐赠基金/专项基金 | Alumni donations help enlarge the university's **institutional endowment fund**. |
| **subsidize higher education** | 补贴/资助高等教育 | Governments ought to heavily **subsidize higher education** for STEM majors. |
| **tertiary institutions** | 高等院校/大学 (替换 university) | **Tertiary institutions** must balance financial sustainability with equity. |
| **adopt a hybrid approach** | 采取双轨制/混合模式 | Policymakers should **adopt a hybrid approach** balancing fees and grants. |

## 2. 篇章论证核心逻辑句型（Grammatical Range）

- **句型 1：让步从句引导平衡观点（Band 7.5+ 开头段杀手锏）**  
  *While the question of whether university tuition should be completely abolished has sparked widespread debate, I believe that financial policies should be differentiated based on students' economic backgrounds.*
- **句型 2：分词伴随结果阻止不良趋势（thereby preventing...）**  
  *Universities must provide full scholarships to students facing financial adversity, **thereby preventing socioeconomic disparity from hindering social mobility**.*
- **句型 3：双重结构递进（not only secure ... but also elevate ...）**  
  *In this way, institutions **not only secure essential operating revenue but also elevate their academic reputation** by nurturing gifted scholars regardless of their wealth.*

## 三、范文精粹高阶词块精讲（本次高光提取）

| 词块 / 表达 | 词性 / 功能 | 中文精准释义 | 考场实战搭配与例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **career prospects and socioeconomic status** | 短语n. | 职业前景与社会经济地位 | Access to higher education profoundly shapes an individual's **career prospects and socioeconomic status**. |
| **the proposal to eliminate ...** | 短语v. | 彻底取缔/废除……的提议 | **The proposal to eliminate** tuition fees entirely has provoked heated controversy. |
| **a blanket policy** | 短语n. | “一刀切”的盲目政策 | Implementing **a blanket policy** overlooks deep wealth disparities across social strata. |
| **overlook** | 及物动词 (V.) | 忽视/忽略了……的关键要素 | Universal subsidy schemes **overlook** the diverse financial realities of individual families. |
| **financial capabilities** | 短语n. | 经济承受能力 / 财务底气 | Tuition requirements should correspond to verified household **financial capabilities**. |
| **academic merits** | 短语n. | 学术资质 / 学业优异表现 | Admissions should reward intellectual talent and **academic merits** above all else. |
| **a differentiated approach** | 短语n. | 差异化/分类对待的折中方案 | **A differentiated approach** reconciles public educational fairness with fiscal sustainability. |
| **household income** | 经济统计术语 | 家庭总收入 | Subsidy eligibility is calculated strictly in accordance with verified **household income**. |
| **scholarly promise** | 短语n. | 治学潜质 / 学术有出息的希望 | Professors seek to mentor promising youths who exhibit exceptional **scholarly promise**. |
| **far more equitable** | 短语adj. | 远为公平合理的 / 更加公允的 | A tiered fee framework offers a **far more equitable** alternative to flat-rate pricing. |
| **underprivileged backgrounds** | 短语n. | 处于社会底层/弱势贫困背景 | Talented applicants from **underprivileged backgrounds** deserve uncompromised institutional support. |
| **impoverished regions** | 短语n. | 经济枯竭与贫困落后地区 | Educational grants must be targeted toward households residing in **impoverished regions**. |
| **local youths** | 代替 students | 当地青年群体 | High tuition burdens render university study virtually inaccessible to **local youths**. |
| **virtually unattainable** | 短语adj. | 实际上几乎高不可攀/遥不可及 | Elite credentials remain **virtually unattainable** for working-class offspring without grants. |
| **left unaddressed** | 短语(条件状语) | 倘若不予解决 / 如果任其恶化 | **Left unaddressed**, rising tuition costs will cement intergenerational wealth stratification. |
| **deprive ... of ...** | 短语v. | 剥夺了某人……的机会/权利 | Financial barriers **deprive** promising students **of** their legitimate right to self-actualization. |
| **academic potential** | 短语n. | 学业潜能 / 学术潜质 | Full scholarships empower underprivileged youths to unlock their latent **academic potential**. |
| **living allowances** | 短语n. | 生活津贴 / 日常开支补贴 | Financial aid packages should combine tuition waivers with monthly **living allowances**. |
| **obstruct social mobility** | 短语v. | 阻碍社会阶层垂直流动 | Prohibitive educational fees severely **obstruct social mobility** across generations. |
| **affluent** | 高阶形容词 (Adj.) | 家境优渥的 / 富足的 (代 rich) | It is entirely defensible to charge full tuition rates to **affluent** applicants. |
| **voluntary philanthropy** | 短语n. | 自愿慈善捐款 / 公益捐赠 | Private universities rely substantially on **voluntary philanthropy** from prominent alumni. |
| **laboratory facilities** | 短语n. | 实验室尖端设备与科研设施 | Adequate funding guarantees that universities can modernize their **laboratory facilities**. |
| **distinguished faculty** | 短语n. | 卓越的师资学者与大牛教授 | Tuition revenue enables top institutions to attract and compensate **distinguished faculty**. |
| **endowment funds** | 短语n. | 大学留本捐赠基金 / 储备基金 | Strategic **endowment funds** insulate universities from economic volatility while sponsoring the poor. |
| **alumni generosity** | 短语n. | 校友的慷慨回馈与解囊相助 | Decades of **alumni generosity** have established massive scholarship trusts for disadvantaged peers. |
| **subsidize** | 动词 (V.) | 财力资助 / 拨款补贴 | Endowments are mobilized directly to **subsidize** tuition fees for impoverished undergraduates. |
| **prosperous** | 高阶形容词 (Adj.) | 繁荣充裕的 (代 wealthy) | **Prosperous** households possess adequate discretionary reserves to fund higher education. |
| **institutional sustainability and social fairness** | 短语n. | 高校运转的可持续性与社会公正双赢 | A hybrid model strikes an exquisite equilibrium between **institutional sustainability and social fairness**. |
