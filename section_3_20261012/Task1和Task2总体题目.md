- **Task 2:** ~40 minutes (at least 250 words)

## Task 1: Academic Report (Two Pie Charts)

> **The pie charts below show the proportion of average household expenditure across six categories in a developed nation in 1995 and 2020.**
> 
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

Write at least **150 words**.

## Task 2: Academic Essay (Discussion & Opinion)

> **Some people believe that the best way to solve global environmental problems is to increase the cost of fuel and encourage individuals to adopt simpler lifestyles. Others, however, argue that technological innovation and international government regulations are the only realistic solutions.**
> 
> **Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least **250 words**.
