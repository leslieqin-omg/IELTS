# 第一部分：Task 1 高分表达（双饼图对比专栏）

针对本次 1995 vs 2020 年家庭开支占比变化，提炼饼图最核心的高分表达与句式。

## 1. 饼图开头改写句型
- **公式 1**：`The two pie charts compare the breakdown of average household spending across six categories in a developed nation between 1995 and 2020.`
- **公式 2**：`The provided graphs delineate the changes in household expenditure patterns over a 25-year period from 1995 to 2020.`

## 2. Overview 概述段黄金句式（抓住最大份额与核心涨跌）
- **句型 A（榜首易主与两极分化）**：
  > *Overall, while Food & Drink and Housing accounted for the vast majority of family budgets throughout the period, Housing overtook Food & Drink to become the single largest expenditure category by 2020.*
- **句型 B（显著收缩 vs 稳步增长）**：
  > *It is noticeable that whereas spending on Clothing and Food & Drink dropped substantially, outlays on Housing and Transport witnessed noticeable expansions.*

## 3. 饼图核心高分词块（占比、主导与增减）
- **占比/构成**：`account for`, `comprise`, `make up`, `represent`
- **最大份额 / 支配地位**：`constitute the largest proportion`, `remain the dominant category`
- **开支 / 预算分配**：`household expenditure`, `budget allocation`, `financial outlays`
- **腰斩 / 缩水**：`halve`, `shrink by more than half`, `diminish considerably`

# 第二部分：Task 2 高分表达（环境治理与科技政策专栏）

针对本次富人生活方式冲击 vs 科技与国际法规协同减排，提炼最权威的学术词块与论证句型。

## 1. 核心高分词块与搭配（Collocations - 严格标注词性性质）

| 词块 / 表达 | 词性性质 | 中文精准释义 | 实战高分例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **curb environmental degradation** | 短语v. | 遏制全球环境恶化 | International cooperation is essential to **curb global environmental degradation**. |
| **lifestyle austerity** | 短语n. | 极简/紧缩的生活方式 | Relying purely on **lifestyle austerity** fails to halt climate change. |

| **financial insulation** | 短语n. | 雄厚的经济缓冲/财务免疫力 | The rich possess the **financial insulation** to absorb soaring fuel prices. |

## 2. 篇章论证核心逻辑句型（Grammatical Range）

- **句型 1：批判性驳论（开头段自然亮明立场）**  
  *While some advocate for higher fuel taxes, such measures disproportionately burden average citizens while failing to deter affluent elites. A truly pragmatic solution must combine technological innovation with international regulatory enforcement.*
- **句型 2：因果内嵌推导（解释为何油价改变不了富人）**  
  *Because these wealthy elites possess the financial insulation to absorb inflated fuel costs, relying exclusively on price mechanisms leaves the root causes of luxury pollution untouched.*
- **句型 3：破折号同位语终极收束（结尾段升华）**  
  *The ultimate imperative is to accelerate scientific innovation in clean energy while instituting binding multinational regulations, ensuring that sustainable technologies become universally accessible and economically viable across all nations.*

### 3. 十大考官级母句骨架（写作时直接往里套内容）

1. **让步 + 局限对比（句 1 杀手锏）**：  
   `While some advocate for [A], such measures merely [解决小问题] while leaving [核心系统/大问题] intact.`
2. **动词短语亮出核心方案（避免使用 I think）**：  
   `A far more realistic and enduring solution lies in [方案名词] reinforced by [制度保障].`
3. **先承认有效，再指出根本不足（Concession 极品论证）**：  
   `This financial deterrent can certainly [达到局部小效果]. However, [某单一手段] alone is fundamentally insufficient to [解决根本危机].`
4. **精确归因对比（rather than）**：  
   `The vast majority of [问题] originate from [主要工业源头] rather than [微观个人源头].`
5. **动名词作主语直击要害**：  
   `Expecting [某微弱措施] to solve [系统性危机] is therefore unrealistic.`
6. **分号连接必要条件**：  
   `Technological innovation cannot succeed in a vacuum; it requires [外部制度支撑] to achieve scale.`
7. **法规驱动因果（When + be compelled to）**：  
   `When regulatory bodies enforce [严格标准], companies are compelled to [投入研发并淘汰旧产能].`
8. **反面假设论证“为什么必须监管”（Without 虚拟语气）**：  
   `Without such global legal frameworks, multinational corporations would simply relocate high-polluting operations to [监管薄弱地区].`
9. **分词伴随结果状语（thereby / -ing）**：  
   `...instituting binding multinational regulations, compelling industries worldwide to transition toward sustainable practices.`
10. **破除两难困境（allow ... without forcing ...）**：  
    `Breakthroughs in clean technology allow societies to [实现目标] without forcing citizens to [牺牲基本生活标准].`
